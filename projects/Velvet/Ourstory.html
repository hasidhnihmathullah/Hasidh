Our Story


The Vision

Velvet Vogue was founded by John Finlo, an ambitious entrepreneur with a passion for the transformative power of fashion.
John recognized that for young adults, clothing is more than just fabric—it is a primary tool for expressing identity and personal narrative.


The Collection

We specialize in a curated selection of trendy casualwear and sophisticated formal wear. From the "Comfort TEE" that anchors your daily look to the "White Shirt" that defines your professional presence, our pieces are designed to be as versatile and unique as you are.
We don't just sell clothes; we offer the accessories and essentials that help you navigate your world with confidence.
