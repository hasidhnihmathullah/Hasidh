02 — HOW A JOB RUNS

Four stages, one crew

The sequence rarely changes — what changes is the scope inside it.

  1. 01

    Walkthrough & Bid

    We assess the site, review your goals and budget, and return a itemized bid within five business days.

  2. 02

    Design & Permit

    Drawings, engineering, and permit filing — we track the approval and handle any plan-check corrections.

  3. 03

    Build

    A dedicated project lead runs the schedule and sends weekly photo updates so you always know what stage you're in.

  4. 04

    Walkthrough & Close-out

    Final inspection, punch list, and a binder of permits, warranties, and material specs for your records.

04 — WHO'S ON SITE

Run by builders, not a call center

  • In-house carpentry, framing, and finish crews
